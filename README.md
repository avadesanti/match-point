# Ava Desanti - match-point

**MATCH POINT** — a single-file canvas tennis game (`match-point.html`). You play your best friend, who is better than you but cracks under pressure. First to 5.

- `← →` move, `Space` toss / swing, hold `↑` for a deep shot or `↓` for a soft drop, `X` returns to `arcade.html`.
- Her name defaults to "Maya". Change `FRIEND_NAME` at the top of the script, or open `match-point.html?name=Sam`.
